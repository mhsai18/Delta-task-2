DArk : Echo Sector

An advanced top-down simulation environment. The entire sector network is hidden in complete darkness. You must navigate using your forward flashlight sweep matrix to eliminate defense drones.

Operational Rules
  • Inverted Deployment: The player resides on the OUTSIDE corridors. All enemies are locked INSIDE the boxes.
  • Airtight Wall Hulls: Rooms are color-coded in solid Green. Projectiles bounce off interior/exterior hulls completely.
  • Aperture Mechanics: Every box has an opening. Bullets can only cross into or out of boxes by passing completely through these Yellow openings!
Control Matrix Mapping
  • [W, A, S, D] Keys: Traverse through grid channels.
  • [Mouse Position]: Aim weapon nozzle and light vector.
  • [Left Mouse Click]: Discharge ricocheting ammunition lines.
  • [Spacebar]: Burn 500L to flash-charge an interactive shield buffer.
